Attribution for the memory multi-session corpus
================================================

The synthetic multi-session memory-stress corpus
(`frontend/e2e/fixtures/memory_multisession_corpus.json`, built by
`scripts/build_memory_multisession_corpus.py`) derives its STRUCTURE and the
ability classes it probes (single-session-user / -preference, multi-session,
temporal-reasoning, knowledge-update, abstention) from:

    LongMemEval: Benchmarking Chat Assistants on Long-Term Interactive Memory
    Di Wu et al., ICLR 2025
    https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval

Our corpus rows are INDEPENDENTLY AUTHORED paraphrases of those ability classes,
not copies of upstream haystacks, so the committed fixture is self-contained.
`scripts/fetch_longmemeval.py` pulls the upstream JSON (Hugging Face
`xiaowu0162/longmemeval`) into the gitignored `cache/` for anyone who wants to
grow the corpus from the real haystacks; that downloaded data is NOT committed.

LoCoMo (persona-drift SHAPE reference only)
-------------------------------------------

The `persona-drift` cases borrow the persona + evolving-preference SHAPE of:

    Evaluating Very Long-Term Conversational Memory of LLM Agents
    Maharana et al., ACL 2024 — https://github.com/snap-research/locomo

LoCoMo is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial). It is used here as a STRUCTURAL
reference ONLY: no LoCoMo text, personas, or dialogue is copied or redistributed.
The persona-drift rows are independently authored (the corpus test suite asserts
the upstream speaker names never appear in the fixture).
