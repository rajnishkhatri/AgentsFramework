- [ADR-0008: Subject-Coach judges — Grader, Grader Judge, and Pedagogy GoalJudge](0008-subject-coach-judges-grader-and-pedagogy.md) — **Accepted 2026-07-01, with conditions** (κ TPR/TNR floor for the answer-leakage detector before its flag is trusted in a gate; the `GoalJudge` `build_graph`-injection lands paired with the judges). - [ADR-0009: Reflexion loop is offline-only for the Subject-Coach (not on the live conversational path)](0009-subject-coach-reflexion-not-on-live-path.md) — **Accepted 2026-07-01.** Evaluates whether to enable the pipeline's **T2 Reflexion** loop for the multi-turn coach and rules **no**: T2 is a *per-task retry* keyed on a GoalJudge task-failure verdict (a coaching turn has no such verdict — the student's reply *is* the loop); it converges toward *the correct answer*, the exact **answer-leakage** behavior the design forbids; it adds 2–3 LLM calls/turn on a latency-sensitive UX with no measured gain; and `reflections` has **no `task_id` guard**, so a prior turn's critique leaks into the next (a latent chat-wide bug, flagged as a separate fix + a hard prerequisite for ever enabling T2 in chat). General principle: inline Reflexion is for checkable single-task runs, not conversational turns. Reflection for the coach lives in the **offline judge layer** (the Pedagogy judge *is* turn-reflection). Grounded in 2026 reflection trade-off research + the Feynman/Oakley/Holt pedagogy (Reflexion rescues the answer; the coach must not). Its one hard prerequisite — the `reflections` cross-turn leak — was satisfied ahead of ratification by ADR-0005-reflections (`efc1715`/`27f1490`), so the ADR ratified clean (no conditions).
