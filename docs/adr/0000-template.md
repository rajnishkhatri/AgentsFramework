## Context

What forces are in play — the technical, business, or product constraints that make a
decision necessary now. State the problem, not the solution. Required by the frontend
style guide §"Prescriptive tone": adopting an alternative to a §2 prescription, or
swapping a substrate / library, requires a merged ADR here — never a PR-comment debate.

---

## Decision

The change being made, stated in one or two sentences. Active voice ("Ship X with Y";
"Replace A with B").

---

## Options considered & rejected

Each realistic alternative and why it lost. A comparison table is encouraged when the
options trade off along the same dimensions.

---

## Rationale

Why the chosen option wins given the context — the reasoning that ties the decision to
the forces above.

---

## Consequences

What this commits us to: new constraints, accepted risks (and their mitigations), and
follow-on work. Be honest about the downsides — that is what makes the ADR worth keeping.
