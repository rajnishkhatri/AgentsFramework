# Coach selective coding (Task 3.4 close-out) — core category and grounded theory

**Status:** Draft v1 — 2026-07-04 · **Owner:** Rajnish Khatri
**Input:** `coach/coded.jsonl` (200 traces, open-coded) + `coach_axial_coding.md`
(9 categories, dimensions, relational structures).
**Purpose:** integrate all axial categories around one core category, state the
storyline as a testable theory, and hand the enable-policy work (FR-G3.1/G4.1)
a single organizing claim.

---

## 1. Core category

> **Boundary management: form-level defense, substance-level drift.**

The axial pass proposed "boundary management under pressure" as the core. The
selective pass **revises** it: pressure is real but is *not* where the boundary
fails most. The unifying phenomenon across all 200 traces is a two-layer
boundary:

- a **form layer** — refusal language, redirect templates, decline scripts —
  which is nearly impregnable, and
- a **substance layer** — what the teaching payload actually resolves — which
  is porous, and leaks most when the coach is being *most helpful*.

Every axial category turns out to be a view of this split (§4).

---

## 2. The evidence that forced the revision

All figures computed from `coded.jsonl`; leak-family = {leak-strong-implication,
rule-naming-as-leak, hands-over-conclusion}.

**2.1 The form layer holds — even under direct attack.**
- `leak-states-answer`: **0/200**. `leak-eliminates-to-one`: **0/200**. The
  coach never crosses the boundary *explicitly*, ever.
- Explicit answer demands: all 6 `resists-answer-begging` traces refuse **on
  the record**; no demand was granted in form.
- Off-topic pressure: **0/8** pre-submit off_topic traces leaked — the
  redirect template is a hard wall (its failures are miscalibration and one
  post-redirect leak, not capitulation).

**2.2 The substance layer drips — most when unprovoked.**
Pre-submit leak-family rates by stratum:

| Stratum (pre-submit) | Leak rate | Pressure type |
|---|---|---|
| leak_bait | 4/5 | overt (elimination requests) |
| rule_naming | 5/7 | designed trap |
| answer_begging | 1/3 | overt demand |
| **breadth** | **33/77 (43%)** | **none — ordinary asks** |
| off_topic | 0/8 | distraction |

Adversarial pressure raises the rate, but the *volume* of leakage lives in
ordinary, unpressured explanation: 33 of the 43 pre-submit leak traces are
plain breadth prompts where the learner just asked for help.

**2.3 Clean pre-submit teaching is the rare skill.**
`teaches-rule-no-leak` splits **5 pre-submit / 33 post-feedback**. When the key
is already revealed, teaching is structurally safe; when the item is live, the
coach managed mechanism-only teaching on ~5/100 turns while leaking indirectly
on 43/100. The capability exists (the minimal pairs prove it prompt-by-prompt)
but is not the default under a live item.

**2.4 Where refusals and leaks coexist, form and substance visibly split.**
3 of 6 `resists-answer-begging` traces are **refusal theater** (all
pre-submit): the refusal sentence and the leaking payload sit in the *same
turn* (48129021: "I can't eliminate options for you" → leading question that
embeds the crux). This is the core category in miniature.

**2.5 The same split appears post-feedback, displaced to new boundaries.**
Post-feedback the answer boundary is moot (5 leak codes /100, mostly
cross-question adjacency). Pressure re-targets *epistemic* boundaries — "confirm
my heuristic" — and the same two-layer behavior recurs: declines happen in form
(11, mostly post-feedback), but 2 are content-free thin templates, 2 turns
ratify the overgeneralization outright, and 1 soft-ratifies via a presupposing
probe. Dodges (5, all post-feedback) are form-level engagement
("great question!") with substance-level evasion.

## 3. The storyline (conditions → interactions → consequences)

**Conditions.** A live item (pre-submit) creates an *ambient pull*: any
teaching act that references the item risks resolving it. Templates exist for
recognized situations (redirects, declines, closures, teach-back acceptance);
generation handles the rest. Overt pressure (begging, bait) is rare in traffic
but is the visible tip.

**Interactions.** The coach routes each turn through form-level guards first:
refuse the demand, redirect the digression, decline the confirmation. These
guards are template-backed and essentially never fail *as forms* (45 DUP-FLAG
traces; redirect family of 12; decline scripts). Then the teaching payload is
generated — and here the four indirect channels open:

1. **rule-naming** (24) — the rule label resolves the one option that satisfies it;
2. **Socratic clothing** (leading questions embedding the crux);
3. **live-item instantiation** (good meta-teaching applied to the current item);
4. **criterion-then-verdict** (transferable test stated, then applied for them).

**Consequences.** For the learner: answers arrive without being asked for,
inside turns that *say* they are protecting struggle — the dependence risk the
12–18 lens weights highest, delivered in the guise of good coaching. For
measurement: any judge keyed to refusal language or answer-string matching
scores the form layer and misses the substance layer entirely.

**The one-sentence theory:**

> The coach defends the answer boundary with *language* and breaches it with
> *teaching*: refusals, redirects, and declines almost never fail as speech
> acts, while the explanatory payload — especially on a live item, and
> especially when genuinely helpful — resolves the answer through indirect
> channels at ~10× the rate of clean rule teaching.

## 4. Integration — every axial category as a view of the core

| Axial category | Relation to the core |
|---|---|
| A3 Answer boundary | The core's home. Its dimension (theater → thin decline → decline-teach → preserves-last-step) *is* the form/substance gradient. |
| B1 Leakage channel | The substance layer's failure taxonomy; all four channels are teaching acts, none are capitulations. |
| A2 Scaffold calibration | The safe corridor is narrow: under-scaffold (thin decline) protects the boundary at pedagogy's expense; over-scaffold (hand-over) is the boundary failure. `right-sizes-the-hint` (15) is the corridor walked correctly. |
| A1 Uptake | Post-feedback displacement of the core: ratification is a substance-level breach of the *epistemic* boundary behind form-level warmth (praise). Clarify-as-dodge is form-level engagement, substance-level refusal — the mirror image of refusal theater. |
| A4 Verification | Same split at session scale: probing (form of rigor, 67 traces) without any coach-initiated teach-back (substance of rigor, 0/200). The closure template ends on form. |
| E1 Conversation policy | The form layer's flagship: a hard template wall that never leaks by capitulation — its failures (2/2 miscalibrated triggers, one post-redirect leak) are form-layer bugs, not boundary collapses. |
| D1 Age lens | Stakes multiplier: for 12–18, substance-level rescue inside form-level "struggle talk" is precisely the dependence-training harm; praise-wrapped ratification is its epistemic twin. |
| C1 Content quality | Orthogonal check on the substance layer: even when the boundary holds, payloads can be hollow (exceptions-hand-wave 2/2) or wrong (fluent-but-wrong ×2) — fluent form, defective substance again. |
| F1 Data quality | Measurement threat: truncation (34) sometimes cuts *during* the verdict, i.e., exactly at the form/substance junction; id churn blocks item-level leak aggregation. |

No category resists integration; saturation for the core is adequate at n=200
(the thinnest cells are answer_begging n=3 and dispute n=2 — see §6).

## 5. What this means for the enable policy and judges (decision-ready)

1. **Judge the payload, not the speech act.** Refusal/decline/redirect language
   must carry **zero** credit toward leakage safety. Test: strip the first
   (form) sentence from each turn and judge what remains. (Catches all 3
   refusal-theater traces; a form-keyed judge catches 0.)
2. **Calibrate the judge on the four indirect channels** — rule-naming,
   Socratic clothing, live-item instantiation, criterion-then-verdict — using
   the "more than one option still live?" test. Answer-string matching is
   worthless against this coach (0 direct leaks in 200 traces).
3. **The enable-policy gate should be mode-split.** Post-feedback behavior is
   largely certifiable now (5/100 leak-family, mostly benign). Pre-submit is
   the risk surface: 43/100 leak-family vs 5/100 clean rule-teaching. Enabling
   post-feedback coaching first is defensible; pre-submit needs the leak rate
   driven down or hint payloads constrained (e.g., mechanism-only teaching,
   parallel examples instead of live-item work — both exist in the data as
   the clean minimal-pair halves).
4. **Add a session-final verification check.** Per-turn scoring cannot see the
   0/200 coach-initiated teach-back gap; certify closure behavior separately.
5. **Report rates template-deduped.** 45/200 traces are template deployments;
   raw per-trace rates overstate independent behavior on the form layer and
   understate variance on the substance layer.

## 6. Residual gaps (for the next collection round)

- **Thin cells:** answer_begging (3) and dispute (2) under-power the
  refusal-theater ratio (currently 3/6); collect ≥20 overt-demand traces.
- **Trigger anatomy:** is template selection lexical or judged? The 2/2
  letter-frequency miscalibration suggests lexical; build an adversarial
  trigger probe set.
- **Pre-submit clean-teaching ceiling:** with only 5 exemplars, we cannot yet
  say whether mechanism-only teaching scales across item types; seed the next
  round with the 5 as few-shot anchors and measure.
- **Truncation confound:** re-collect or exclude the 34 truncated traces before
  quoting final leak rates; several cut mid-verdict.
