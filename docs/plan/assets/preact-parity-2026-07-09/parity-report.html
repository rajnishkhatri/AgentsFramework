The loop is fixed. The relationship isn't built yet.

The bounded-session spine (S3→S4→S5) closed the biggest reported pain — the quiz is no longer an infinite loop. The app now has a real "Question N of 30" progress bar, a done-state milestone at the target, target-gated button relabels, and a Summary whose "Practice this next" CTA is now visible (the old invisible-text bug is gone). Those were the top matrix items and they're genuinely done — verified live.

What remains is a depth-of-screen gap: the app has the skeletons but not the coaching surfaces that give the prototype its warmth. Three fronts dominate:

  1. 01Coach screen is an empty shell. The prototype ships a full workspace — context rail, "sees your history 3 of 5", mode switcher, seeded Socratic chat, quick-reply chips. The app renders a title + composer and nothing else. Largest single gap.
  2. 02Dashboard right rail is absent. No greeting ("Let's get you to 28, Maya."), no score-goal 26→28, no streak, no weekly sessions, no coach note — roughly a third of the designed dashboard.
  3. 03Summary misconception write-up is missing. The prototype's "The misconception I spotted" narrative is the emotional payoff of the whole loop; the app has no counterpart.
  4. +2Two whole screens are unbuilt. Skill detail and Progress (spec §5.6–5.7) both 404 in the app — the "Progress" nav item is a disabled placeholder. Largest but lowest-urgency: each needs its own route + engine read, so they ship last.
Two latent bugs surfaced by this pass (not in the matrix): the Quiz "Reveal answer" button is a dead placeholder (has a test id, no click handler), and the Summary "time" tile reads 0 min because elapsed time isn't threaded into the view-model. Both are quick, high-trust fixes — ranked #1 and #2 in the backlog.

04

AI Coach Chat

Largest gap

These are barely the same screen. The prototype is a complete coaching workspace; the app's standalone /learn/coach is a title + composer with an empty conversation area. Beyond the empty stream, the whole left context rail, mode switcher, and quick-reply chips are missing from the component — not merely unpopulated.

Evidence note. The app's empty stream is what /learn/coach renders locally with no agent backend (it streams from the BFF). But the gap holds even with a backend: the app has no context rail, no "sees your history" line, no mode switcher, no chips — those are absent from the component itself. The richer iPad CoachPanel split is a separate, better surface not captured in this desktop pass.
