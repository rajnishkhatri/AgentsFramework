## 1. Framing (please confirm the subset)

We are building your **Adaptive-Lesson** artifact as a real screen: `/learn/skill`, the full **learn → miss → revise** loop for one English skill. We adopted your **3-context block composer** (`newSkill` / `returning` / `refresher`, Lesson-Block-Schema) as the shipping surface, and we are **deferring the 6-mode SCQA beat engine** (§2.2 sequence table + §5 `selectMode`).

The reason is a hard data boundary: our outer-loop engine emits only **`mastery`, `missTag`, and `due` schedule** (your own `AL-15`/`AL-24` v2 signals). It does **not** emit `feelsProblem`, `prefersWorkedExamples`, `prefersVisual`, or `justMissed` — which are exactly the inputs your `selectMode` ladder branches on (`struggling`, `worked`, `annotated`, `diagnostic`). Without those signals we cannot faithfully select 4 of your 6 modes, so we bind to the 3 contexts, which need only mastery/miss/due.

**Confirm:** is the 3-context composer a faithful subset of your design — i.e., do the contexts preserve the pedagogical intent of the modes we're dropping — or do we lose something load-bearing (a beat, an invisibility guarantee, a learning-science effect) by not shipping the full mode engine? We keep your **invisibility contract's** a11y half (`AL-25`/`AL-AC-8`: role color always carries a text label) regardless.

## 2. Decisions we need from you

### D1 — The context selector is net-new; give us the state → context boundaries
Your artifact ships an **executable** selector for the **6 modes** only (`selectMode`, §5, priority ladder + `AL-16` default-to-`new` + `AL-17` `requested`-wins). For the **3 contexts** it ships only a manual preset tab-switcher and one prose sentence ("in production the context is a decision from learner state"). So `selectLessonContext({mastery, misses, due}) → newSkill | returning | refresher` is a contract **we must author from scratch**. We need your intent on the boundaries:

- **(a)** At what **mastery %** and/or **due-miss count** does a learner flip `newSkill → returning`?
- **(b)** Does the mere **presence of a misconception tag** force `returning`, or only a tag on a **currently-due** miss?
- **(c)** Does `refresher` require **zero due misses** (mirroring your `masteryPct >= 80 && dueMisses == 0 → refresher` rule), and what mastery floor?
- **(d)** Safe **default** when state is unknown / first exposure (your `AL-16` analog)?

**Why it matters:** this function decides which surface every learner sees on every entry to `/learn/skill`; it is the top of the build and blocks the E1a spec.
**Our lean:** first-exposure/unknown → `newSkill` (AL-16 analog). `refresher` when `mastery ≥ 80 && due == 0` (your one transferable numeric anchor). `returning` when there are due misses. Undecided: whether an untagged due-miss still qualifies for `returning`, and the exact `newSkill → returning` mastery threshold. We only get to use mastery/missTag/due — not the mode signals.

### D2 — Does end-on-resolution (`AL-13`) still govern the block composer?
`AL-13` ("a self-contained lesson ends on the `rule` resolution; only `refresher` may end on the `tension` caution") and `AL-11` (a dropped beat stays reachable as drill-down) are **beat laws**. `AL-23` explicitly stacks beats **above** blocks and maps `rule → rule + annotatedExample + workedExample`. But you never propagate `AL-13` through that beat→block mapping — and at the block layer, all three contexts end on a **rail** block (`accuracyStat` / `coachEntry`), while `rule` **leads** `newSkill` (index 0) and sits 3rd in `returning`. So no context "ends on rule."

The real echo is in your own `newSkill` preset reason string: *"teach forward … ends on a win"* — the **main** column ends on `completionTry`/`selfExplain` before the rail stats.

**The decision:** should we lift **"the main zone must end on the resolution/win block"** into the composer as an explicit main-zone ordering guard, or retire `AL-13` as **beat-only** and let block order be governed solely by the context recipe?
**Our lean:** keep it as a main-zone guard for `newSkill` (ends on the doable win); let the rail carry stats/coach independently.

### D3 — `completionTry`: illustrative mock, or a real graded rep?
`completionTry` is a mini-question in shape (`{sentence, promptHint, choices:[{text, correct}]}`), but your composer renders it **inert** — plain buttons, no click/grade/record, the `correct` flag never reaches the DOM. That commits to "static illustration." But E1 is the **full** learn→miss→revise loop, and this block is in `newSkill` (E1a — **not** deferrable).

**The decision:** is the "doable win" **(a)** static practice that must **not** record an attempt or move mastery, or **(b)** a **real graded rep** whose click records an attempt and advances the spaced-repetition schedule? If (b): does a wrong pick change what the lesson shows next?
**Why it matters:** our `Scheduler.review()` is the **sole** writer of skill mastery state. If the teach-scaffold win writes mastery, an in-lesson warm-up starts moving the FSRS schedule — almost certainly not what you intend, but you authored the beat, so you decide.
**Our lean:** (a) — local teaching scaffold, no attempt recorded, mastery untouched. Confirm.

### D4 — `selfExplainPrompt`: where does the learner's free text go?
Your `selfExplainPrompt` collects free text ("explain it in your own words **before** any instructional explanation" — the self-explanation effect). In your composer the textarea is **unbound** (text goes nowhere). It's in `newSkill` (E1a — not deferrable). Disposition is a **pedagogy** call, not just wiring:

- **(a)** discarded after prompting (pure metacognitive nudge, no persistence),
- **(b)** shown back to the learner locally as their own note, never stored/scored,
- **(c)** handed to the Socratic coach as opening context.

**Our lean:** (a) or (b). We'd avoid (c) in E1a since it couples the block to the coach handoff, which we're staging for E1b.

### D5 — Where does `blocks[]` live — extend `Tutorial`, or a new `LessonPlan`?
Your artifact models a lesson as **structured, ordered, typed data** at two nested levels: `beats: Record<BeatId, Beat>` above, each beat rendering as one or more of the **9 typed blocks** (each with `zone` main/rail + a semantic `role`), selected by `context`. Our persisted lesson wire type is **flat prose** (`body_md` + `examples[]`) with no blocks/zone/role/context/beats. This touches our cross-language wire kernel (an ADR-triggering change).

**The decision (design intent, not our plumbing):** is the composed, ordered **block+beat** structure meant to be the **persisted, authored shape** of a lesson (the coach stores ordered `blocks[]`/`beats` per skill), or is it a **render-time composition** the client assembles on the fly from separately-authored raw pieces (a one-line rule, examples, a misconception tag, an accuracy series)?
**Our lean:** render-time composition from raw pieces — the durable artifact stays close to our existing content, and the composer orders at render time from learner context. Tell us if the block/beat order is itself authored content you expect to persist.

### D6 — `misconceptionCallout` body/fix: verbatim tag, or synthesized aggregate?
`returning`/`refresher` lead with `misconceptionCallout` `{label, body, fix}`. Your composer demo shows an **aggregate** body ("Across 4 missed items you removed commas… especially when the clause starts with *which*") + a `fix` heuristic ("run the removal test").

Our data can back label/body **only** as the **single newest missed item's verbatim, author-written, one-line misconception tag** — free-text, present on ~27% of items, absent on the rest, shown **verbatim** (so it cannot leak the answer). This is exactly how our Session-Summary screen already renders it today. We **cannot** produce your aggregate body (that needs counting misses **and** clustering free-text tags into a theme — no controlled taxonomy exists), and we have **no source at all** for the `fix` heuristic.

**The decision:**
- **(1)** Is a **single-miss verbatim tag** as body acceptable for v1 (callout omitted when the newest miss is untagged), or is the **cross-miss aggregated diagnostic** essential to the block's purpose — in which case it waits for a reviewed tag-generation pipeline (our N-5 E1b)?
- **(2)** Should `fix` be in the v1 block at all, given no field backs it and any authored corrective text must pass our answer-leakage lint?

**Our lean:** ship (1) verbatim single-miss tag now; **drop `fix`** from v1 until authored corrective text with provenance exists.

### D7 — `accuracyStat` on a first-exposure surface has no data
`accuracyStat` wants a headline `value` (%) + a **6-session per-skill accuracy series** (`bars`). But it sits in **`newSkill`** — the **first-exposure** surface (E1a, not deferrable), where by definition the learner has **zero prior sessions** for that skill, so even a real series is empty. Separately, our only per-skill scalar is an FSRS **mastery/retrievability** estimate, which is **not** answer-accuracy (this exact confusion is a known dashboard bug).

**The decision:**
- **(1)** On **first exposure**, should `accuracyStat` be **(a)** omitted from the `newSkill` recipe, **(b)** render an explicit "no history yet" empty state, or **(c)** show a forward-looking placeholder?
- **(2)** On `returning`/`refresher` where history exists, must `value` be **true answer-accuracy** over a real session window (a new aggregation we'd build), rather than the mastery/retrievability scalar we already store?

**Our lean:** (1a) omit on first exposure — a 6-bar trend on a skill with no history is dishonest. (2) yes, true accuracy for returning/refresher. (Building the bars from our single-fill progressbar idiom is our engineering call, no answer needed.)

### D8 — `dueChecklist`: whole skills, or intra-skill micro-topics?
`dueChecklist` renders `items[]` as specific due micro-topics **within one skill** (your example: four comma sub-topics for one Commas skill). Our scheduler only knows due-ness at **whole-skill** granularity (one `due_at` per skill; no sub-skill decomposition), so we cannot populate an intra-skill list from engine state.

**The decision:**
- **(A)** `items[]` = whole **skill names** that are due ("Commas", "Subject-verb agreement") — we can drive this directly from `due_at`, and the micro-topics in the mock were illustrative; **or**
- **(B)** it must be **intra-skill micro-topics** — in which case those are **authored static content per skill** (a fixed checklist you supply), **not** derived from learner data?

And: should the `cta` ("Clear them now") deep-link into a **drill pinned to that one skill**? (We're adding the scheduler skill-pin to support it.)
**Our lean:** (A) whole due skills, driven from `due_at`, cta deep-links to a skill-pinned drill.

## 3. Input we'd value (we have a lean; want your read)

### I1 — `misconceptionCallout` fallback when we can't assert a "pattern"
Independent of D6's verbatim-vs-aggregate call: because a missed item is tagged only **~27%** of the time and tags are free-text (no clustering into a recurring "pattern"), the eyebrow **"Your pattern · X"** and an aggregate body are unsupportable most of the time. When the block **can't** honestly assert a recurring pattern, which fallback preserves your intent?

- **(A)** reframe to a single-item, lower-confidence eyebrow ("On your last miss …") with body from that one item's tag,
- **(B)** degrade to a neutral **miss-count** evidence line ("4 recent misses on this skill") — no pattern claim, no fix,
- **(C)** keep "Your pattern · X" **only** when a real recurring tag exists, hide the block otherwise.

And in each case: what should the **fix** line say when we have no authored corrective heuristic?
**Our lean:** (A) when the newest miss is tagged, (C) hide-when-untagged as the floor; **no fix line** absent authored text.

## 4. How we're binding your design (FYI — no answer needed)

Fidelity target is your `role → style` resolution (including `accentDashed → transparent bg, dashed border` and the `AL-25` no-card-backgrounds rule); we precompute `role → resolved token` in a pure translator upstream, because our components hold no domain logic.

- **`accuracyStat` chart.** No multi-bar chart primitive exists in our repo; we hand-build the 6-bar chart from our single-fill `progressbar` idiom, with the numeric % always rendered alongside per `AL-AC-8`.
- **`misconceptionCallout` needs a fallback because only ~27% of items are tagged** (seed bank 0%); see D6/I1. The verbatim single-miss binding matches a screen we already ship.
- **`completionTry` / `selfExplainPrompt` / `coachEntry` are inert in your composer** — we treat their affordances as design intent and wire the backing actions (D3/D4 and the coach handoff below).

- **Invisibility contract scope.** `AL-18/19/20` (hide S/C/Q/A letters, Situation/Complication terms, ordering codes) govern the **SCQA beat engine** we're deferring — there's no SCQA engine on our side to hide, so those bind nothing in this build, and your `adaptive-lesson.spec.js` targets mode-only artifacts (6 mode tabs, "Tuned for you" strip, "start here" opener) we don't produce. **We author our own block-composer acceptance criteria** (correct block list per context, main/rail zone partition, unknown-tag skip, `role → color` resolution, number-always-shown).
