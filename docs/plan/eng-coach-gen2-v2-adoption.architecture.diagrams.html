1. Use-case diagram (broken down)

Source: §2.2 · the single diagram is split into three by actor cluster: learner serve paths, authoring/curation, and A/B experiment assignment.

1a · Learner-facing serve paths

UC-1..UC-4 — the four moments a learner hits the seam. Quiz Engine owns the no-LLM path (UC-1/UC-2); LLM Coach owns the chat paths (UC-2..UC-4); BFF is the mode authority for free-ask and post-feedback.

flowchart LR
  L([Learner]) --- UC1(UC-1 Request hint\nbefore any pick)
  L --- UC2(UC-2 Unstick after\nwrong pick)
  L --- UC3(UC-3 Free-ask chat)
  L --- UC4(UC-4 Post-feedback\nreview)
  QE[[Quiz Engine]] --- UC1
  QE --- UC2
  LC[[LLM Coach]] --- UC2
  LC --- UC3
  LC --- UC4
  BFF[[BFF mode authority]] --- UC3
  BFF --- UC4

1b · Authoring & curation

UC-5 (Phase-1 pilot ladders on reviewed Gen1 stems) and UC-6 (Phase-2 Gen2 batch curation, deferred). Author/Reviewer is the only actor that can flip reviewed:true; Curation Pipeline runs the emit scripts + validators.

flowchart LR
  A([Author/Reviewer]) --- UC5(UC-5 Author + promote\nchoice-conditional ladder)
  A --- UC6(UC-6 Curate + promote\nGen2 item)
  CP[[Curation Pipeline]] --- UC5
  CP --- UC6
  UC5 -. fuels .-> UC2ref(UC-2 wrong-pick\nladder, see 1a)
  UC6 -. deferred to Phase 2 .-> UC6ref(blocked on B1-B5)

1c · A/B experiment assignment

UC-7 is meta: it selects which ladder pack UC-1/UC-2 serve. Phase-1 dogfood → student A/B; assignment randomizes the ladder pack only (never viewport/device, per Direction 2b parity).

flowchart LR
  E([Experimenter]) --- UC7(UC-7 Assign\nladder-pack A/B)
  UC7 -. selects pack for .-> UC1(UC-1 no-pick hint\nsee 1a)
  UC7 -. selects pack for .-> UC2(UC-2 wrong-pick unstick\nsee 1a)
